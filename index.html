Previous Events

◇ Event Image

CV Masterclass - From Campus to Corporate was a hands-on career-readiness workshop built to help students bridge the gap between university and the professional world. The session covered what actually matters in today's job market; from crafting a resume that stands out, to decoding how corporate recruitment really works. Participants also walked through interview preparation techniques and strategies for adapting to a professional environment, leaving with practical, actionable tools for their next career move.

CV Masterclass - From Campus to Corporate

◇ Event Image

The Marketing Challenge 2026 brought theory to life, pushing students into live marketing scenarios where creativity, communication, and strategic thinking were tested in real time. Partnering with Street Origins (Fragrance World), the event featured the brand's own Brand Manager as judge, giving participants a genuine taste of industry-level evaluation. Teams battled through three high-impact rounds; Brand Recall, a 90-Second Skit, and an Investor Pitch, each designed to sharpen a different skill, from spontaneous thinking to structured persuasion. Team Velocity took home the win and the AED 300 prize, with participation certificates awarded across the board.

The Marketing Challenge

◇ Event Image

Bizz Quiz returned for its second edition, organized by AEP's newly elected council with an upgraded question bank and a sharper competition format. Building on the flagship quiz's foundation, this edition brought fresh, current business questions and refined judging criteria to raise the bar even further. The competition closed out with a high-stakes buzzer round and a hard-fought battle for the overall title, with cash prizes up for grabs for both, ending the season on a high-energy note!
